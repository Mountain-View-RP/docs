# Notable Historical Figures

The people remembered by Mountain View shaped its laws, secrets, and scars.

Some are celebrated publicly. Others survive only in family stories, sealed records, or supernatural memory.

## What belongs here

Use this section for figures whose choices still affect the present.

Include:

* Founders, leaders, activists, and cultural figures
* Practitioners, protectors, and rivals from the hidden world
* People whose legacy creates a current ally, threat, mystery, or obligation

## Writing a figure

Each profile should establish:

* **Who they were** — their background, role, and public reputation
* **What they changed** — the event, institution, or magical force they shaped
* **What remains** — their living legacy in modern Mountain View

{% hint style="info" %}
Use real historical people with care. Keep documented history distinct from the setting's fictional magical elements.
{% endhint %}
