## Biography

Robyn was born in England in 1400. He arrived in Mountain View with the First Fleet, then built influence within the growing settlement.

Over centuries, he helped shape Mountain View's local government and its supernatural council. His ownership of **The Cube**, a Downtown nightclub, gives him a public-facing base and a private source of information.
